---
name: kpi-semis
description: Use when sourcing Pepper semiconductor or memory industry KPI evidence and possible price/volume drivers for Position Growth.
---

Read `docs/harness/POSITION_GROWTH.md`. Source ASP, bit shipments, capacity/utilization and mix by company, business line, fiscal period and currency/unit. Quote the filing section/paragraph behind each measured input; do not treat company guidance, commentary or an industry index as realized sales. Attribute changes to P and Q only when the bases reconcile with revenue; otherwise leave the proxy descriptive or use the two-part revenue/cost fallback from `pqc-decompose`. Mark absent, stale and unverified values explicitly. For cyclical companies, compare through-cycle profit with current profit and keep incremental ROIC/PEG out of verdicts.
