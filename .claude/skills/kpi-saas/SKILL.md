description: Use when sourcing Pepper SaaS recurring revenue, customer, ARPU or retention KPIs as possible Position Growth drivers.
---

Read `docs/harness/POSITION_GROWTH.md`. Record company definitions for ARR/MRR, customers, ARPU/ARPA, churn/retention and recognition, plus source filing section, paragraph, as-of and period. ARR is not booked revenue; net retention and cohort changes cannot be substituted for customer counts. Decompose P×Q only when consistent period/base/units permit a revenue reconciliation. Otherwise report verified growth evidence and use the revenue/cost fallback from `pqc-decompose`; do not invent missing KPI values or let AI calculate financial labels.
